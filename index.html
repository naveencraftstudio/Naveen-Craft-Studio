About Naveen Craft Studio

Naveen Craft Studio is a design studio in Chennai that turns ideas into spaces people love to live and work in. We design interiors, exteriors, and complete building layouts, bringing together function, comfort, and style in every project.

Our process starts with understanding how you live or work. We then develop plans, visualize the finished space in 3D, and refine the design with you until every detail feels right. Clients come to us for a clear, transparent process from first sketch to final render.

Whether it is a single room, a new home, or a full building facade, we approach each project with care, precision, and a commitment to making your vision a reality.

Our Services

From the layout of a room to the look of a building's exterior, we provide design services for homes, apartments, villas, and commercial spaces.

Interior Design

Spaces designed around how you live, with a focus on comfort, storage, and style.

  • Living, dining, and bedroom design
  • Modular kitchens and wardrobes
  • Lighting, materials, and furniture selection
  • Office and commercial interiors

Exterior Design

Building facades and outdoor spaces that make a strong first impression.

  • Elevation and facade design
  • Color, texture, and material planning
  • Compound walls and gates
  • Landscape and outdoor living areas

2D Design

Accurate plans that serve as the foundation for every project.

  • Floor plans and room layouts
  • Electrical and furniture layouts
  • Space planning and measured drawings
  • Plans for approvals and construction

3D Design

Photorealistic renders so you can see the finished space before work begins.

  • Interior 3D renders
  • Exterior 3D renders
  • Walkthroughs and multiple view angles
  • Design revisions shown visually
